## 1. Pilot Objectives & Operational Principles

The primary objective of the LAWMA-360 v2 field-validation pilot is to stress-test the **six core engineering safeguards** and **11 operating modules** under realistic Lagos operational friction (grid instability, telecommunications dropouts, wet-season access bottlenecks, and high-volume commercial throughput) before statewide deployment.

### Non-Negotiable Operational Principles
1. **Zero Financial or Legal Risk in Non-Production:** All early testing and simulated disruptions must use synthetic ledger accounts, test properties, and consented fleet units. No real financial deductions, tax withholdings, police actions, or mobile court summonses may be generated from unvalidated pilot scripts.
2. **Deterministic Fallback Readiness:** Every field exercise must maintain an active, briefed manual fallback team with dual physical sign-off capability before any hardware or network simulation is initiated.
3. **Data Integrity & Immutability:** All edge and field transactions must be captured with cryptographic provenance. Re-tests and corrections append superseding entries rather than altering historic audit logs.
4. **Configurable Policy Isolation:** Every policy-sensitive variable (fuel reimbursement rates, scale variance caps, complaint cure hours, escrow allocation percentages) must run as versioned runtime configurations, clearly labeled as simulation defaults pending formal statutory approval.

### Phase 0: Non-Production Sandbox & Hardware Rig Emulation (Days 1–7)
- **Scope:** Laboratory and staging environment. Connect physical edge terminals (RS-232 digitizers, OBD-II/CANbus units, mobile enforcement tablets) to sandboxed backend services.
- **Key Test Procedures:**
  1. *Scale Jitter & Outage Injector:* Inject noisy analog load-cell voltage feeds, simulate sudden serial cable severance, and verify edge local SQLite queuing.
  2. *Cryptographic Signature Verification:* Verify ECDSA SHA-256 signatures generated by edge units under high-rate load (50 tickets/minute).
  3. *GPS Blackout Emulation:* Drive telematics units through RF-shielded enclosures; verify zero-loss sensor packet storage and monotonic sequence catch-up.
  4. *Idempotency Stress Run:* Replay identical HTTP outbox batches 10 times against the settlement ingestion gateway; verify exactly one processed record.
- **Exit Gate:** 100% automated test pass on all P0 safeguard unit and integration suites; zero memory leaks or signature validation failures.

### Phase 1: Isolated Facility & Single-Ward Ground Testing (Days 8–21)

#### Track A: Weighbridge & TLS Scale Pilot (Simpson TLS & Olusosun)
- **Location:** Simpson Transfer Loading Station (Inbound & Outbound Scales) and Olusosun Landfill Gate 1.
- **Test Procedures:**
  1. *Planned WAN Cutoff:* Disconnect the optical fiber/LTE uplink at Simpson TLS for 4 continuous hours during peak morning compactor arrivals (07:00–11:00).
  2. *Edge Ticket Generation:* Marshals process incoming test compactors using local touchscreens. Verify tickets issue in <15 seconds with offline QR payload and valid hardware sequence counters.
  3. *Dual-Scale Reconciliation:* Test compactor gross weighing on Scale 1, simulated mud accumulation, and empty tare weighing on Scale 2. Verify dynamic tare calculation:
     $$	ext{Net} = 	ext{Gross} - 	ext{Tare} - \Delta_{	ext{mud}}$$
  4. *WAN Restoration & Resync:* Re-enable network uplink; observe automated queue draining, timestamp verification (capture time vs. server ingest time), and duplicate payload rejection.

#### Track B: Fleet Logistics & Telemetry Pilot (Alimosho Beachhead)
- **Location:** Alimosho Local Government (Ward B - Egbeda/Idimu corridor).
- **Test Procedures:**
  1. *Live Route Tracking:* Equip 6 accredited AWAMN compactor trucks with retrofitted telematics devices. Monitor 05:00 AM depot departure, route geofence compliance, and bin dwell times.
  2. *Queue-Triggered Diversion:* Inject a simulated 90-minute turnaround jam at Solous Landfill. Dispatch controller transmits an automated diversion order to Simpson TLS.
  3. *Driver Interaction & Fuel Compensation Audit:* Driver terminal displays reroute notification, audio prompt, and revised map route. System logs driver acknowledgement time, extra kilometers traveled, and posts calculated diesel reimbursement credit to the test escrow ledger.

#### Track C: Cadastre & Tenement Master Pilot (Surulere Multi-Flat Compound)
- **Location:** Surulere LGA (Selected pilot streets: Bode Thomas & Adeniran Ogunsanya blocks).
- **Test Procedures:**
  1. *Doorstep DUID QR Tagging:* Survey teams tag 50 multi-family tenements, issuing individual Dwelling Unit IDs (DUID) linked to occupant contact profiles.
  2. *Split-Billing Generation:* Issue individual monthly waste service bills to Flats 1 through 6.
  3. *Tenancy Transition & Arrears Ring-Fencing:* Simulate tenant move-out on Flat 3 with ₦18,000 in simulated arrears. Register new incoming tenant on Flat 3. Verify clean ledger generation for the new tenant, while historical debt remains isolated in an owner/transition review status without blocking collection service.

- **Exit Gate:** Simpson TLS edge processes ≥120 consecutive trucks without scale downtime; Alimosho trucks record 100% route telematics breadcrumbs; zero arrears crossover between tenement occupants.

### Phase 2: Inter-Agency Operational & Field Exception Workflows (Days 22–35)

#### Track D: Enforcement & Mobile Court S.84 Mock Trial (LAGESC / KAI)
- **Location:** LAWMA Special Environmental Court, Oshodi.
- **Test Procedures:**
  1. *Field Violation Capture:* Environmental health officers discover a simulated illegal commercial black-spot dump in Mushin.
  2. *Evidence Package Generation:* Officer uses enforcement tablet to capture 4 geotagged, timestamped photos, barcoded waste manifest tags, and audio notes. Tablet seals package with SHA-256 digest and device TPM credentials.
  3. *Automated S.84 Certificate Assembly:* System compiles the evidence dossier, appends system audit trail logs, and drafts an Evidence Act Section 84 Compliance Certificate for the designated IT Forensic Officer.
  4. *Mock Judicial Review:* Presiding magistrate inspects digital exhibits. Defense counsel challenges exhibit integrity; system executes real-time hash verification. Inject one deliberately modified image file; verify system flags hash mismatch and rejects the corrupted item immediately.

#### Track E: Ecotox Leachate Monitoring & Hazmat Quarantine (LASEPA / MOD-07 / MOD-08)
- **Location:** Olusosun Landfill Leachate Piezometer Station & Hazardous Waste Inspection Bay.
- **Test Procedures:**
  1. *Sensor Threshold Exceedance:* Inject elevated COD/BOD/electrical conductivity test signals into piezometer telemetry stream. Verify immediate MOD-07 threshold alert and automatic escalation to LASEPA joint portal within 120 seconds.
  2. *Biohazard Manifest Interdiction:* Deploy a test truck carrying labeled non-hazardous industrial training waste with an invalid or expired hazardous electronic manifest token.
  3. *Automated Gate Lock & Quarantine Escort:* Dumpsite boom gate denies entry; system triggers an automated quarantine alert, routes truck to the isolated hazardous inspection bay, and logs chain-of-custody transfer.

#### Track F: Night Market Waste Evacuation (Mile 12 & Ketu Markets / MOD-09)
- **Location:** Mile 12 International Market perimeter.
- **Test Procedures:**
  1. *Bulk Union Escrow Validation:* Aggregate market waste volume billed against the Market Traders Association bulk escrow balance.
  2. *05:00 AM Clean-Street Verification:* Concessionaire compactor teams operate between 23:00 and 04:30. At 04:45, fixed surveillance cameras and roving supervisor telemetry capture street cleanliness status. System validates completion before the 05:00 AM milestone, releasing concessionaire operational credit.

- **Exit Gate:** 100% hash verification pass during mock court challenge; zero hazmat gate breaches; successful automated verification of market clearance before 05:00 AM.

### Phase 3: Fintech Clearing, Settlement Rehearsal & DR Failover (Days 36–45)

#### Track G: Friday Automated Settlement Rehearsal (MOD-05)
- **Location:** Central Financial Operations Room, LAWMA HQ / Virtual Clearinghouse.
- **Test Procedures:**
  1. *Full Weekly Clearing Run:* Aggregate 50,000 synthetic verified pickup events and 450 weighbridge scale tickets across pilot zones.
  2. *Waterfall Split Execution:* System calculates gross earned revenue and executes the simulation waterfall: 70% PSP operations, 20% Section 102 Council Fund, 10% LAWMA regulatory fee, deducting 5% LIRS Withholding Tax.
  3. *Shortfall Stress Test:* Artificially withhold 35% of incoming collection gateway cash. Verify system triggers **Shortfall Mode**: halts arbitrary prorations, protects statutory taxes and base operator fuel/wage allowances, logs deficit balance, and generates an auditable State Treasury Office reconciliation brief.
  4. *48-Hour Citizen Dispute Cure Hold:* Inject 25 verified missed-collection citizen complaints from Lekki Phase 1. Verify system restricts dispute holds strictly to the affected 20% invoice component, issues cure dispatch tasks to the assigned PSP, and releases funds upon audited remedial collection verification.

#### Track H: Sovereign Infrastructure Disaster Recovery & Failover (MOD-11)
- **Location:** Primary Datacenter (MainOne MDXi Lekki) & Disaster Recovery Datacenter (Alausa).
- **Test Procedures:**
  1. *Primary Gateway Disruption:* Simulate catastrophic WAN/routing drop at the primary cloud ingestion endpoint during peak data intake.
  2. *Failover Activation:* Monitor DNS failover and edge traffic rerouting to the secondary onshore DR site. Verify recovery time objective (RTO < 60 seconds) and recovery point objective (RPO = 0).
  3. *Zero-Duplicate Settlement Audit:* Run complete database consistency check across primary and secondary transaction outboxes; confirm zero duplicated payout vouchers or orphaned records.

- **Exit Gate:** Reconciled settlement balances match to the exact Kobo across all ledgers; dispute holds release strictly upon verified remedial telemetry; zero data loss during full failover drill.

## 5. Pass/Fail Quality Gates (Entry & Exit Criteria)

A test track or phase fails if any of the following non-negotiable boundaries are breached:

```
[Phase Entry Gate]
  ├── All test accounts pre-funded with synthetic test tokens
  ├── Hardware load-cells and OBD-II units calibrated within 30 days
  └── Manual fallback marshals physically positioned on site
         │
         ▼
[Phase Execution Gate]
  ├── Edge Ticket Processing: Max 20 seconds/truck, zero unhandled exceptions
  ├── Telematics Integrity: Zero GPS fabrication; explicit gap logging
  ├── Settlement Accounting: Aggregate credits == Aggregate debits (Zero Kobo variance)
  └── Evidence Forensics: 100% detection rate on intentionally corrupted digital files
         │
         ▼
[Phase Exit Gate]
  ├── Formal review by RACI Accountable Authority
  ├── Signed verification report by Technical Lead & Operations Director
  └── All logged defects prioritized (Zero open Sev 1 / Sev 2 defects)
```

### Critical Stop-Work Thresholds
1. **Financial Discrepancy:** Any unexplained variance >₦0.00 between clearinghouse inflows and escrow allocations immediately halts the settlement module.
2. **Weighbridge Queue Gridlock:** If offline scale ticketing causes truck queues exceeding 15 vehicles on an external public arterial road, the emergency bypass procedure is triggered immediately.
3. **Evidence Tampering Vulnerability:** If a modified digital image passes Section 84 verification without throwing a signature error, the entire enforcement pipeline is locked down for root-cause remediation.

## 7. Rollback & Abort Procedures

If a test phase violates critical safety or operational thresholds, the following deterministic rollback steps must be executed:

1. **Weighbridge Scale Rollback:** Disengage edge digitizer touchscreen; switch to certified dual-signoff manual logbook; stamp each vehicle manifest with the unique manual emergency code `EMERG-SCALE-[DATE]-[SEQ]`; notify Central Dispatch to limit hourly gate allocations.
2. **Fleet Telemetry Rollback:** Revert compactor drivers to standard static physical route sheets; notify zonal inspectors to perform manual visual bin-clearance audits at designated route checkpoints.
3. **Fintech Clearinghouse Rollback:** Freeze automated payout triggers; isolate the clearing ledger; revert to verified previous-week batch voucher settlement under dual sign-off from LAWMA Director of Finance and State Treasury representative.
4. **Platform Core Rollback:** Point edge terminals back to the known stable primary API endpoint; if database migration errors occur, execute automated point-in-time recovery (PITR) to the pre-test snapshot within 15 minutes.
