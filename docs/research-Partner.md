## 1. Executive Summary & System Diagnosis

Lagos relies on **400–450 accredited PSP operators** to execute primary household and commercial waste evacuation, yet those SMEs sit at the center of Lagos's deepest operational squeeze: **6–7 months of unpaid household bills**, **3-day dumpsite queues**, **rising diesel & crew wages**, **narrow unpaved inner streets**, and sudden **licence withdrawals** (6 franchises terminated or withdrawn under MD/CEO Dr. Muyiwa Gbadegesin and Hon. Comm. Tokunbo Wahab). Meanwhile, the Ministry/LAWMA explicitly mandates PSPs to **digitalize cash collections, data collation, and household enumeration**.

Auditing `LAWMA SmartWaste360_Partner` and `LAWMA-360-partner-app` shows a responsive multi-route operations console (`/`, `/requests`, `/operations`, `/fleet`, `/customers`, `/finance`, `/messages`, `/security`, `/settings`, plus `EvaluationAccessWall` / `ScreenSecurityShield`), **but exposes six critical product and architectural flaws** that keep it from working in Lagos's real PSP economy:

1. **Premature Escrow Release Bug in `store.tsx`:** In `acceptRequest(pickupId, crewId, window)` (`src/lib/store.tsx` lines 149–152), accepting/scheduling a request immediately releases `reserved` funds and writes `"Partner credited ..."` *before* the truck has rolled or emptied a bin! Although `completeStop` separately increments `earningsUncaptured`, this contradicts resident protection rules and exposes the platform to the exact "billed/charged without pickup" distrust that destroyed PUMAU/Visionscape.
2. **Per-Pickup Request Queue Contradicts LAWMA Monthly Ward Franchises:** `/requests` treats PSP haulage like on-demand gig orders (`Requested` -> `Scheduled` -> `Completed`) rather than **contracted monthly route zones** (e.g., *Every Friday*) plus optional extra pickups, and omits multi-flat compound split accounts.
3. **Zero Mid-Mile TLS (`FAC-AGE`) or Disposal Queue (`DS-SOL` 100/day) Visibility:** PSPs complain most bitterly about **3-day landfill queues** and breakdowns at Simpson/Oshodi/Agege TLSs—yet `/fleet` only watches corridor rules (`speedLimitKmh: 50`, `maxLoadPct: 100`, `maxPingGapMin: 10`, `inspectionDays: 90`, `hours: [6, 22]`) without live **Solous II (100 compactors/day)** slot booking, **Agege TLS** turnaround metrics, or downloadable intake weight receipts tied to 80/20 remittances.
4. **Compactor-Only Fleet Blindness (Missing Tricycle Micro-Collectors):** `fleet.ts` models only standard trucks, ignoring narrow inner streets across Alimosho (*Egbeda, Ikotun, Ipaja*) and *Orile Agege* where large compactors cannot enter—leaving residents dependent on unapproved cart pushers unless PSPs manage **Tricycle Micro-Collectors** (`TRI-*`) feeding mother compactors/TLSs.
5. **No Regulatory Status (`Active`, `Probation`, `Suspended`, `Revoked`) or Capacity Scorecard:** PSP owners have no warning before compliance termination and no digital workflow to hand over or assume **interim ward coverage** (such as taking over *Papa Ashafa II*).
| **Disconnected Customer, Sync & Operator Identity** | Partner accounts in `store.tsx` are scoped via static local area strings (Ikeja/Yaba/Ajah/Lekki) with no live sync to `waste-scan-mate` (`Operator`) or `LAWMA v2` (`Workspace`); additionally, `LAWMA v2`'s `experience.js` `serviceAPI` conflicts with `Resident`'s `/api/public/messages` endpoint. | Reconcile sync onto `/api/public/messages` (`hub-contract.ts` v2 with `OPERATOR_APP_KEY` + short-lived scoped tokens). |

**Additional Field Realities Surfaced During Audit:**
- **Tag Provisioning & Bin Lifecycle (`TAG-*` -> `CMP-*`):** Current `/customers` creates bin orders (`120L/240L/360L`) without specifying how low-cost painted gate stencils or laminated QR stickers are provisioned for Alimosho's unpaved inner lanes before `Operator` can verify scans.
- **Residual Cash Collections & Working-Capital Crunch:** Field agents still collect occasional cash from unbanked elders; without an **Agent Cash-to-Voucher Deposit** workflow, digital ledgers miss revenue. Simultaneously, SME PSPs struggling with diesel & wage inflation need **bankable verified SLA/Remittance exports** for working-capital loans or compactor micro-leasing.
- **Rainy-Season Access Lockouts:** During heavy May–Oct floods (`2.2m–2.7m` rainfall), impassable streets need verified **`flood-access`** grace states so PSPs are not penalized by automated Saturday-noon escrow freezes.

## 4. Alimosho–Orile Agege Pilot Implications for Partners

- **Dual Haulage Reality:** PSPs operating in *Agbado/Oke-Odo, Ayobo/Ipaja, Akowonjo–Egbeda,* and *Orile Agege (`Papa Ashafa II`)* experience **Agege TLS (`FAC-AGE`) check-in -> weighbridge -> stock receipt**; PSPs in *Ikotun/Igando, Egbe/Idimu,* and *Mosan-Okunola (Gowon Estate)* tip directly at **Solous II (`DS-SOL`)** within the **100 compactors/day** schedule window.
- **Compound Enumeration Priority:** Over 65% of Alimosho residential buildings are multi-tenant blocks/compounds; onboarding households without **per-compound split billing** guarantees high nominal customer counts with 60%+ non-payment rates.
- **Interim Ward Takeover Readiness:** Seeded backup PSP partner demonstrates how an `Active` Grade-A partner in Alimosho steps into `LCDA-ORA-PA2` (*Papa Ashafa II*, ex-*Guestimate Environmental*) under LAWMA `"Move work"` with inherited paid-customer protection.
