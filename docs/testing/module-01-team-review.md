4. Link a Tier 1 room to a unique LASRRA sample ID. Monthly tariff stays ₦3,500. Tier 2 receives 45-day grace and priority dispute eligibility; no cash rebate.
5. Record an occupancy change with inspection evidence. Incoming tenant starts at zero; former debt and any refundable credit remain isolated for liability/refund review. No automatic landlord liability.
6. Collection tab: shared-bin service continues until a manager confirms bag stock, pickup date, resident notice and safety. Approve a sample plan, issue a QR bag to a paid-up room, view the QR, then paste its sample token into Record collection with a receipt reference. Verify it becomes Collected; re-use must not create another collection.
7. Enumeration tab: mobilisation requires GPS plus gate-photo reference and duplicate check. Record ₦50. Performance requires a verified room payment and proof; record ₦150. Neither stage can be paid twice. Existing legacy payments remain paid, not re-issued.
8. Obalende compound → Notices & court: prepare referral, record lawful service evidence and docket, then record signed-order evidence with legal review. No automatic sealing, utility blocking, bank freeze or live court filing.
9. Prepare evidence certificate; download the JSON. Verify the SHA-256 digest against its exact snapshot. It remains unsigned and requires a responsible officer’s legally compliant attestation.
10. Reload: room changes, payment references, commission records and collection receipts persist. Check phone width and dark mode; tables scroll inside their container.

## Approvals / unresolved production dependencies
- Legal authority, respondent liability, approved summons form and service methods; scope and execution of any court order.
- Alternative collection capacity and sanitation safeguards for every household, including unpaid rooms; QR bag procurement, distribution and actual PSP scanning.
- Commission appropriation, evidence capture/independent verification, actual payments and duplicate/fraud controls.
- LASRRA grace, dispute-priority and civic-benefit policy; identity checks are not live verification.
- Approved LGA/LCDA register and location boundaries; current GPS checks are a sample bounding box, not a cadastral boundary test.
- Live account allocation, transaction reconciliation, legally attested certificates and tamper-resistant audit storage remain deferred.
