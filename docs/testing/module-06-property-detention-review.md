# Module 06 safeguards 4 and 2 follow-up

Dummy-data browser rehearsal, 14 November 2026. Proposed legal protocols require LAWMA review before operational use.

## Property and judicial diversion

- Separate cart register with physical tags, seizure references, linked cases and explicitly unestablished ownership. Initial carts and all new records are dummy.
- CRUSH-ORD- destruction references require a named magistrate/court and property schedule. A different named practice official verifies the order before crusher queue entry.
- Preservation/appeal holds block queuing and destruction. Destruction requires the matching cart tag, crusher safety confirmation, different named witness, video/certificate reference and scrap receipt. Records do not operate machinery.
- Court owner subpoenas retain the alleged cartel evidence basis without treating it as established ownership/guilt. Service evidence precedes court return, including attendance/non-attendance or ownership not established. No subpoena is actually sent.
- Cart-to-trike requires an available matching cart-pusher case, first-offender history reference, voluntary consent and judicial diversion direction. Referral → cooperative enrolment → training → dummy trike handover are separate saved stages. A trike serial cannot be used twice.
- Diversion reserves the old cart; it does not create a destruction order, release custody, close prosecution or dispose of the original cart.

## Detention follow-up

- Existing original-arrest 18-hour Sev-1, 20-hour alarm and 24-hour release/video requirement retained and browser-retested.
- Added explicit matching-case blocker for plea, sentence and case closure while confirmed custody remains unresolved at or beyond 24 hours. The check runs on opening the action and submitting an already-open form. Invalid original arrest times fail closed for confirmed unresolved custody.
- Charge filing and recording completed release/video hearing remain available subject to existing evidence/booking gates, so the blocker does not prevent lawful resolution.
- Acknowledgement, booking, reload and property diversion cannot clear the custody requirement. This is a rehearsal blocker, not an actual constitutional custody authorisation.
