## Limits

All records are browser-local dummy rehearsals. Weather, optical/radar sensors, BLE, GPS, photos, telco ingress, messaging, banks, Treasury, court authority and physical equipment are not connected. A public dummy hashing key and browser storage do not protect real informants or establish legal evidence admissibility. Official names are practice identifiers, not authenticated independent officials. Council polygons, limits, rates and statutory references require LAWMA review before operational use.

The skip calculation includes the 11m³ capacity divisor missing from the dossier's compact formula; this is a dimensionally consistent rehearsal calculation, not an approved engineering model. QA/silence/cutoff clocks are tested as rule boundaries; browser tests do not wait 24 or 48 hours. Staff acceptance and external-system connections remain deferred.
