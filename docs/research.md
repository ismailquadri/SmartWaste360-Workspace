# Regulatory Research Report: Authority Control Room & Clearinghouse Integrity

**Document Version:** 2.0 (System-Specific Research)  
**Target Application:** `LAWMA v2` (Authority Workspace & Public Portal)  
**Research Scope:** Operational audits of LAWMA Headquarters Control Room, Agege TLS weighbridge, and Solous II disposal facility.

---

## 1. Key Regulatory Findings

### 1.1 The Phantom Weighbridge Ticket Problem
- **The Fraud Vector:** Under manual paper ticketing, unscrupulous truck drivers colluded with weighbridge clerks to log fake tipping tickets without dumping waste, or overstated truck gross tonnage to fraudulently inflate trip subventions.
- **System Cure:** Automated digital load-cell bridges with optical barrier axle-sensors and automated license plate recognition (ALPR). Weight entries are locked upon load-cell stabilization and pushed directly into the immutable clearinghouse ledger.

### 1.2 Dumpsite Chokepoints & Public Outcry
- **The LASU-Igando Gridlock:** In past years, compactor queues at Solous II stretched over 3 kilometers along the LASU-Igando road, trapping commuters in 5-hour gridlocks. Drivers dumped waste on road shoulders out of exhaustion.
- **Policy Intervention:** Hon. Commissioner Tokunbo Wahab upgraded Solous II with perimeter concrete fencing, improved internal access roads, and imposed a strict **100 compactor/day cap**.
- **System Cure:** A dynamic 45-minute Turnaround SLA timer. If queue density exceeds threshold, dispatch reroutes trucks to Agege TLS or Olusosun, preventing queue buildup.

### 1.3 The Fourth Schedule Constitutional Tension
- **Inter-Agency Friction:** Local Government chairmen (Conference 57) frequently cite the Fourth Schedule of the 1999 Constitution, arguing that domestic waste management is an exclusive local government function.
- **Regulatory Solution:** The platform integrates a 5% regulatory revenue allocation to host LGAs and provides local council environmental officers with dedicated audit dashboards, aligning state regulation with local governance.
