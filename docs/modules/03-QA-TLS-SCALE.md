### Q05: Why does the system give an exit tare "mud allowance" only between May and October, and why does it credit the hauler instead of penalizing them?

#### The Critical Challenge
During the peak of the Lagos rainy season (June, July, August, September), torrential downpours turn the unpaved access tracks and tipping faces of Solous II (Igando) and Epe Landfills into deep, viscous swamps of red laterite clay. When a 25-ton compactor drives across the tipping face to discharge refuse, wet laterite clay, heavy gravel, and saturated sludge stick inside the dual-wheel tire treads, wheel wells, hydraulic suspension linkages, and chassis undercarriage.
When the empty truck returns to the outbound weighbridge to record its tare weight, it carries between **400 kg and 850 kg of wet laterite mud**.
Under the fundamental billing equation:
$$\text{Net Mass (kg)} = \text{Gross Mass} - \text{Tare Mass}$$
An artificially inflated tare mass directly reduces the calculated net waste:
- A truck enters with 22,000 kg gross.
- Genuine empty truck tare is 12,000 kg (True Net Waste = 10,000 kg).
- Outbound tare with caked laterite mud is 12,600 kg.
- Calculated Net Waste without allowance = $22,000 - 12,600 = 9,400\text{ kg}$.
The honest AWAMN private hauler is cheated out of **600 kg of genuine waste collection payment** on that single trip. Over 30 monthly trips, a single compactor loses 18 metric tons of credited haulage (over ₦250,000 in lost revenue). In past years, this led to furious hauler strikes, protests at the LAWMA headquarters in Ijora, and haulers dumping refuse on highway medians rather than entering muddy landfills.

#### Analysis of Alternative Options
1. **Year-Round Fixed Tare Assumption:** Storing the truck's factory tare weight in a database and never weighing the empty truck on exit.
   - *Why it fails:* Haulers deliberately enter with empty toolboxes and spare parts, then load scrap iron, used tires, or heavy demolition rubble inside the landfill to weigh out heavy and steal recyclable materials, or enter with a half-full diesel tank and exit with an empty tank. Fixed tare creates massive operational blind spots.
2. **Penalizing Haulers for Muddy Trucks:** Refusing to weigh trucks until drivers scrape off the mud with hand shovels.
   - *Why it fails:* Manual scraping takes 45 minutes per truck. A queue of 80 compactors backs up onto LASU-Isheri Road within two hours, paralyzing western Lagos traffic.

3. **The Wheel-Wash Reset Interlock:** If the facility operates an automated high-pressure underbody wheel-wash facility and the truck passes through the wash cycle (`washed === true`), the mud is physically washed away and the allowance resets immediately to **$0\text{ kg}$**.

   $$\text{Adjusted Tare} = \text{Tare}_{\text{measured}} - \text{Mud Allowance}$$
   $$\text{Net Mass} = \text{Gross} - \text{Adjusted Tare} = \text{Gross} - \text{Tare}_{\text{measured}} + \text{Mud Allowance}$$

An unwashed truck weighing out with 12,600 kg tare after a 35 mm rainstorm has its tare adjusted to $12,600 - 600 = 12,000\text{ kg}$, restoring the true 10,000 kg net waste payload. ### Q06: How do you catch haulers who weigh in with 2,000L of stormwater leachate in their compactor sumps and dump it outside before tare weighing?

#### The Critical Challenge
Lagos solid waste contains high moisture content (over 65% organic putrescible food waste and tropical rainwater). Compactor trucks are equipped with sub-chassis leachate collection tanks (holding 1,500 to 2,500 liters) designed to collect liquid squeezed out of refuse during hydraulic compaction, preventing toxic black liquid from dripping onto public roads.
Corrupt operators discovered a lucrative scam: before entering the landfill, the driver fills the leachate tank with 2,000 liters of muddy stormwater from a roadside ditch. The compactor rolls onto the inbound weighbridge: the gross weight is inflated by **2,000 kg**.
Inside the facility yard, before proceeding to the outbound scale, the driver pulls the manual drain valve lever, dumping 2,000 liters of putrid liquid directly onto the yard gravel or into the roadside drainage ditch. The truck then rolls onto the tare weighbridge completely empty. The calculated net waste is artificially inflated by 2,000 kg, generating ₦20,000 in fraudulent hauler payout while severely contaminating local soil and groundwater.

#### Analysis of Alternative Options
1. **Manual Inspection of Drain Valves by Gate Guards:** Ordering security guards to check valve handles.
   - *Why it fails:* Valves can be opened via hidden pneumatic switches inside the cab or drained discreetly while the compactor tailgate is raised at the tipping face. Guards cannot monitor 500 trucks daily in muddy conditions.
2. **Locking Valves with Physical Padlocks:** Sealing valves with plastic zip-ties or padlocks.
   - *Why it fails:* In hot tropical conditions, full leachate sumps overflow into the truck's electrical wiring and brake lines if not drained when full. Padlocks corrode in hours due to sulfuric and organic acids in leachate.

### Q07: How do you stop drivers from leaving rear tandem wheels off the scale platform ("partial platforming") to artificially manipulate weights?
